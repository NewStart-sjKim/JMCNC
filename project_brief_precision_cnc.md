# Project Brief & PRD: CNC Precision Machining Website

## 1. Project Overview
**Brand Name:** PRECISION CNC
**Industry:** Industrial Manufacturing / CNC Machining
**Target Audience:** Global industrial procurement officers, engineers, and manufacturing partners.
**Primary Objective:** To showcase high-precision technical capabilities and drive lead generation through a streamlined quotation inquiry process.

---

## 2. Design Vision
**Design System:** Industrial Precision System
**Visual Style:** Professional, clean, and technical. High contrast (Slate/Navy) with "Safety Orange" as the primary action color.
**Core Principles:**
- **Precision:** Clean lines, consistent spacing, and technical typography.
- **Reliability:** Heavy use of high-quality industrial imagery.
- **Efficiency:** Mobile-first, conversion-oriented layout.

---

## 3. Sitemap & Navigation
**Primary Navigation:**
- Home (메인)
- Product Introduction (제품소개)
- Quotation Inquiry (견적문의)
- Notice (공지사항)
- Company Introduction (회사소개)

## 4. Functional Requirements (MVP)

### 4.1 Global Navigation
- **Top App Bar:** Branding, quick "GET QUOTE" CTA.
- **Navigation Drawer:** Full access to all sitemap links with iconography.

### 4.2 Main Page (Home)
- **Hero Slider:** High-impact visual carousel showcasing facility and precision engineering.
- **Core Capabilities Section:** 3-column grid (on desktop) or vertical list (on mobile) featuring:
    - CNC Turning (고정밀 자동선반 부품 가공)
    - 5-Axis Milling (복잡한 형상의 다면 가공)
    - Precision Assembly (모듈 조립 및 품질 검사)
- **Quotation Inquiry Form:** Integrated form for lead capture (Name, Company, Email, Project Details, File Upload).

### 4.3 Quotation Inquiry System
- Support for CAD/PDF file uploads.
- Structured input fields for material, quantity, and deadline requirements.

### 4.4 Footer
- Legal links (Privacy Policy, Terms).
- Certifications (ISO 9001).
- Direct support contact.

---

## 5. Technical Specifications
- **Device Support:** Mobile-first responsive design (Desktop versions to be generated).
- **Framework:** HTML5 / Tailwind CSS.
- **Typography:** Geist (Primary technical font).
- **Icons:** Material Symbols (Industrial/Manufacturing set).

---

## 6. Future Scope (Phase 2)
